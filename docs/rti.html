Escalation ladder (complaints and non-response)

Typical BMC chain

  1. Ward engineer / department head
  2. Assistant Municipal Commissioner of the ward
  3. Deputy Municipal Commissioner (zone)
  4. Additional Municipal Commissioner
  5. Municipal Commissioner

Typical order only. Confirm the current structure and contacts on the authority's website. Other bodies (MMRDA, other municipal corporations) differ.

RTI route

  1. RTI application to the Public Information Officer (PIO)
  2. First appeal to the First Appellate Authority
  3. Second appeal to the State Information Commission

Also consider the authority's grievance channels and the elected ward representative in parallel. Keep copies and proof of posting or filing of everything.
