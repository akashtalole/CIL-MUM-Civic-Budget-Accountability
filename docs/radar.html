Tender Risk Radar

Explainable screens that rank tenders for human review: single bidders, near-estimate awards, tight bid spreads, repeated extensions, frequent co-bidders, possible contract splitting and monsoon timing. Click any row to see why it scored as it did, and the innocent explanations for each signal.

Synthetic data. The tenders, contractors and patterns here are generated, including deliberately planted collusion and splitting patterns used to test the detectors. The page shows the method works on known patterns, and says nothing about any real tender or firm.
A score is a triage aid, not evidence. It points reviewers to documents worth requesting (see RTI Copilot), and never alleges wrongdoing.
