Where does the money go?

Prototype tools built for Claude Impact Lab Mumbai (Civic Budget & Accountability). They help citizens see public spending in Mumbai and the wider Mumbai Metropolitan Region, track whether works are delivered, and ask the right questions of the right people.

This is a prototype. Charts marked real snapshot use public tender counts. Charts marked synthetic use generated sample data to demonstrate the method and say nothing about real tenders or companies.

Principles

Indicators, not accusations

A flag means "worth a closer look" and always shows the numbers behind it. Single-bidder tenders and extensions often have innocent explanations.

Public data, sanctioned access

Sources are public portals, official exports and RTI replies. Captchas and access controls are not bypassed.

Cite and date everything

Each record should link to its source and show when it was last updated.

Independent

Not an official BMC, MMRDA or Government of Maharashtra service.
